Story

Story outline (five acts)

The outline decides who and where each part of the video is about: the setup, rising action, climax, aftermath and new normal. Edit anything, and the clips are chosen to match.

Face recognition runs on this device (nothing is uploaded). Anyone who appears in at least two clips becomes a character you can pick to sort by.

AI looks at frames from each clip and notes shot size, faces, expression and subject, so the story can open wide, push in to faces and eyes as the music builds, and pull back at the end.

Tap a block on the timeline to swap that shot.

Director techniques

On-screen text
Each platform gets its own opening line: 2 to 6 words, on screen from the first frame, in the top third of the safe zone and held long enough to read twice. Music-video exports get a lower-third title card instead. Titles are checked against ones you’ve used before.

Your own title (used when automatic titles are off)

Ending (full-length videos)

Progress border

At the edgeFurther in
Phones taller than the video (most phones now) fill the screen and trim a little off both sides in YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels. The default puts the line just outside YouTube’s like and share buttons on a typical phone; move it in if your phone still hides it.
SubtleSolid
A fine line with curved corners runs around the picture and completes as the video finishes. On vertical videos it sits in from the sides, so phones that trim the edges of a Short still show all of it.

Connect ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok

One extra step: the version of this app hosted in Claude can only talk to Claude. Save a copy to your computer, open the saved file in Chrome or Edge, and the other reviewers work there with your own API keys.

Saved keys are locked

Keys go straight from your browser to that company over an encrypted connection, and nowhere else. Saved keys are encrypted (AES-256) with your passphrase, which is never stored. For extra safety, create a separate key for this app with a monthly spending limit, and delete it if you stop using the app.
