# Beat Splicer: privacy policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Beat Splicer edits your video clips to your music on your own device. This page explains what leaves your device and why.

## What stays on your device

- Your clips, photos and songs. They are read, analysed and turned into videos on your phone. They are never uploaded by the app.
- The videos you make, which are saved to your phone (Movies/Beat Splicer) and only go elsewhere when you share or upload them yourself.
- Your settings, projects, the app's notes on what is in your clips, and the record of which footage earlier videos used.

## What is sent, and only when you use the feature

- **Lyrics lookup**: the song's title and artist go to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to find synced lyrics.
- **Downloads of the app's models**: the picture-and-word model and, on capable devices, the beat model are downloaded from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. Nothing about you or your clips is sent.
- **AI reviewer** (optional, with your own key): small contact-sheet pictures of your edit and a text summary go to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI), under that provider's terms.
- **YouTube** (optional, in the web version): with your Google sign-in, the app reads your channel's statistics and uploads videos you choose. Google's privacy policy applies.
- **Problem reports** (only when you press Send report): your description, the app version, device model, the last error, recent steps and settings go to the developer as an issue in a private GitHub repository. Your clips and songs are not included. An email address is included only if you type one.
- **Purchases**: handled by Google Play. The app learns only which upgrades you own.

## What the app does not do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no selling or sharing of data.

## Children

Beat Splicer is not directed at children under 13.
